Part 6 • Explain Your Design Choices

My Color Analysis

Explain how your color choices relate to color theory and how you would use each palette in a website.

Palette 1

Harmony: Complementary

Why I selected these colors: I selected blue and gold because they create strong contrast. The blue gives the palette a professional feeling while the gold helps important elements stand out.

Mood: This palette creates a confident, energetic, and professional mood. The contrast between the blue and gold makes the colors stand out.

Website Usage: I would use blue for the main sections and navigation of a website. Gold could be used for buttons, links, and other important elements that need attention.

Palette 2

Harmony: Analogous

Why I selected these colors: I selected purple, blue, and pink because they are close together on the color wheel. This allows the colors to work together while still giving the website some variety.

Mood: This palette creates a creative, energetic, and modern mood. The colors have a bright appearance that can make a website feel more interesting and expressive.

Website Usage: I would use purple and blue for larger areas such as headers and sections. Pink could be used as an accent for buttons, links, or other elements that need attention.

Palette 3

Harmony: Triadic

Why I selected these colors: I selected green, brown, and purple because they create a three-color combination with noticeable differences between the colors. This gives the palette a lot of visual variety.

Mood: This palette creates a bold, creative, and natural mood. The green gives the palette a natural feeling while the purple adds more creativity and the brown provides balance.

Website Usage: I would use green as the main color for sections and navigation. Purple could be used for secondary elements, while brown could be used for accents and supporting content.
